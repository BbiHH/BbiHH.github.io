## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at **Hunan University**, advised by [Da Cao](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/people/caoda). Since November 2026, I have been visiting the School of Computing and Information Systems at **Singapore Management University (SMU)**, hosted by [Lizi Liao](https://computing.smu.edu.sg/faculty/profile/6261/liao-lizi-6261) and supported by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).

I received my master's degree from Minzu University of China and my bachelor's degree from the University of South China.

## Research Interests

**How can vision-language models learn where to look when they need more visual evidence?** I am interested in *Thinking with Images*, where a model can crop or zoom into part of an image and use the resulting view to continue reasoning. I am exploring how reinforcement learning can teach models to select informative regions and how to measure whether a closer look helped answer the question.

My earlier work focused on multilingual vision-language learning, including adapting English-centric models to other languages and transferring visual question-answering capabilities through knowledge distillation.
