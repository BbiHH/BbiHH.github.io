## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at **Hunan University**, advised by [Da Cao](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/people/caoda). I am currently visiting the School of Computing and Information Systems at **Singapore Management University (SMU)** under the supervision of [Lizi Liao](https://computing.smu.edu.sg/faculty/profile/6261/liao-lizi-6261), supported by a **China Scholarship Council (CSC)** scholarship.

I received my master's degree from Minzu University of China, where I was advised by [Yu Weng](https://xingong.muc.edu.cn/info/1051/1150.htm). I received my bachelor's degree from the University of South China.

## Research Interests

My interests include **visual reasoning**, **Thinking with Images**, and **reinforcement learning**. I am particularly interested in how vision-language models can learn to use visual tools to gather evidence during reasoning.
