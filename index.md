## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at **Hunan University**, advised by [Da Cao](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/people/caoda). Since November 2026, I have been a visiting Ph.D. student at **Singapore Management University (SMU)**, hosted by [Lizi Liao](https://computing.smu.edu.sg/faculty/profile/6261/liao-lizi-6261) in the School of Computing and Information Systems. My visit is supported by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).

I received my master's degree from Minzu University of China and my bachelor's degree from the University of South China.
